## Facebook and communities · English

📻 Into amateur radio and using a Mac? I'm developing RadioWorkbench, a native macOS and Apple Silicon application that brings reception, decoding and signal exploration into one workspace.

It works with microphone, USB, virtual-cable and WAV audio, with profiles to help set up audio from SDR++. Modes include CW/Morse, RTTY, PSK31, FT8/FT4, SSTV, weather fax, NAVTEX, packet/APRS and more. It also offers digital voice through an optional engine, station guides, satellite queries and passes, propagation conditions, HF planning and terrain-based VHF/UHF estimates. You can log receptions, keep audio and SigMF I/Q, and explore your reception map.

✨ Version 0.16.0 adds a Spanish/English interface and six Settings sections: language and session, audio, appearance and performance, library, data and alerts, and connections and diagnostics. New options include waterfall controls and lower-power rendering, a configurable library location and manual backups, connection presets and configurable data refresh.

Special thanks to **G4ZJH** for the contribution and the idea of offering both Spanish and English. Thank you for helping improve the project!

This is an experimental project for people who enjoy radio on a Mac. Testing, feedback, bug reports and new ideas are all welcome.
